Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Clerk — authentication and user account management

cortecs.ai — AI Inference Provider

Submitted benchmark tasks are processed through cortecs.ai, an EU-based AI inference platform. cortecs.ai operates according to the following principles:

  • EU Sovereign — cortecs.ai builds on EU-native clouds, keeping data fully governed under EU regulations, ensuring maximum protection and complete control over information.
  • Data Residency — cortecs.ai guarantees that data never leaves Europe. Even when using proprietary models not hosted on EU-native clouds, EU data residency is maintained.
  • GDPR-Compliant — cortecs.ai software is designed to comply with European privacy laws by default.
  • No Training — submitted data is never used to train AI models. cortecs.ai enables exclusion of providers that do not adhere to this policy.
