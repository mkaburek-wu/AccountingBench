tax fin. acc. mgmt. acc. interp. of law calculation
The low overall calculation score is largely driven by task composition rather than by a uniform inability to solve calculations. Of the 54 calculation items, 45 are labeled as open_text, 8 as open_numeric, and only 1 as multi_choice. Performance is strong on the multi_choice item (~95%) and moderate on open_numeric (~59%), but very low on open_text (~13%), which pulls down the entire calculation category. These 45 open-text calculation tasks are taken from a textbook and rely on extracting values from tabular content; it seems likely that many models struggle to read and structure this table information reliably.
